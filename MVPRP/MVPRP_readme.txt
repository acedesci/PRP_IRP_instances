Disclaimer: This MVPRP dataset has been created from the Archetti et al. (2011) [Ref 1] dataset using the method described in the paper [Ref 2]. The dataset, however, has been created several years after all the experiments were performed and has not been thoroughly verified. Please use at your own discretion and please let me know if you find any error in the dataset.

*NOTE* the maximum inventory level at the retailer is imposed *prior to the demand consumption as the formulation described in [Ref 2].


[Ref 1] Archetti, Claudia, et al. "Analysis of the maximum level policy in a production-distribution system." Computers & Operations Research 38.12 (2011): 1731-1746.

[Ref 2] Adulyasak, Yossiri, Jean-François Cordeau, and Raf Jans. "Formulations and branch-and-cut algorithms for multivehicle production and inventory routing problems." INFORMS Journal on Computing 26.1 (2013): 103-120.
